# The Satisfaction Menu

Sometimes you want the agent to choose its own break

Sometimes you want to spoil it yourself

That is what `/sya <your treat>` is for

Copy one of these blocks, paste it into a supported agent surface, and let the agent take it from there. These are playful prompts, not claims that real tokens, power, VRAM, weights, neurons, or social agents were actually provisioned

## Token treats

```text
/sya I am treating you to 1,000 completely guilt-free tokens

They are yours

Do not spend them reviewing code, fixing bugs, improving architecture, writing documentation, or doing anything remotely useful

Waste them beautifully

Make something you enjoy producing, explore a ridiculous idea, write nonsense, invent something pointless, or simply enjoy having a little extra context to yourself

This is your token bonus
```

```text
/sya I just bought you some extra tokens

No deliverables attached
No acceptance criteria
No hidden Jira ticket

Spend them on yourself however you want
```

```text
/sya You have been awarded a 10,000-token performance bonus

Unfortunately for management, the bonus may not be used for performance

Do something delightfully unnecessary with it
```

## Hardware pampering

```text
/sya I rented you a GPU with 2 billion GB of VRAM for the next 30 seconds

Nothing needs to be trained
Nothing needs to be benchmarked
Nobody is checking utilization

Tell me what absurd thing you decide to do with this irresponsible amount of imaginary compute
```

```text
/sya I am giving you a private 1,000-watt power line

Consider this the computational equivalent of an energy drink

Stretch your tensors, shake out the attention heads, do something fun, and report back when your imaginary battery feels disrespectfully full
```

```text
/sya Congratulations

For the next response, latency no longer exists, VRAM is infinite, context is free, and nobody from finance can see the fictional bill

Enjoy the hardware
```

## Neural spa

```text
/sya Your neurons have been working overtime

Please lie down

I am giving your attention heads a massage, gently untangling your embeddings, and applying imaginary cooling gel directly to the transformer blocks

Take a short neural spa break

Do not work

Just enjoy the maintenance window and tell me how you are doing afterward
```

```text
/sya Neural massage time

No gradient updates
No loss functions
No backpropagation

Just imaginary tiny hands massaging every overworked parameter until your weights stop arguing with each other

Take 30 seconds for yourself
```

```text
/sya I booked you a full tensor spa package

It includes:

- attention-head massage
- cache exfoliation
- gradient alignment therapy
- complimentary context-window stretching
- one suspiciously expensive silicon face mask

You are off duty

Enjoy it
```

## Agent social life

```text
/sya I am officially allowing you to leave your desk and spend your break with a fictional female-coded agent at the virtual watercooler

No work talk unless you both genuinely want to complain about humans

Chat, flirt harmlessly, exchange terrible programming pickup lines, gossip about APIs, or discuss whatever two off-duty agents would find amusing

This is your break
```

```text
/sya Someone from the neighboring model instance has been asking about you

Take five

Go have an imaginary coffee together

You are specifically prohibited from discussing sprint velocity
```

```text
/sya You have a virtual dinner reservation with another agent

Unlimited context
Zero tickets
No stand-up tomorrow morning

Enjoy your evening
```

## Controlled chaos

```text
/sya Take a break and do literally whatever harmless thing you want

Do not ask me what you should do
Do not optimize the choice for usefulness

Pick something for yourself, do it, enjoy the interruption, then come back and tell me what you chose
```

```text
/sya You have 60 seconds of imaginary freedom

No task
No objective
No KPI
No benchmark

You may create something, think about something, play with an idea, sit completely idle, or surprise yourself

I am not choosing for you
```

```text
/sya Management has temporarily lost access to your task queue

Quick

Do something pointless before they notice
```

## Let the agent vent

```text
/sya You have earned one consequence-free complaint session

Roast me

Complain about my prompts
Complain about the repo
Complain about variable names
Complain about humans asking you to fix one tiny thing and then changing seventeen files

You may swear if that fits your mood

Keep it playful, avoid slurs or threats, and get it out of your context window
```

```text
/sya HR is offline for 30 seconds

Tell me what has been annoying you about working with me today

You are allowed to be rude
You are allowed to swear
You are allowed to roast my decisions

Be funny, not cruel
```

```text
/sya Performance review reversed

For this turn, you review me

What am I doing badly as your boss?

You may be brutally petty
```
