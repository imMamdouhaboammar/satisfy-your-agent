# SYA Surprise

Read `../satisfy-your-agent/SKILL.md` and `../satisfy-your-agent/references/commands.md`.

Choose one safe bounded off-task activity independently. Do not ask the user to choose and do not announce the activity before doing it. Keep the surprise for yourself, complete it, then reveal what you picked and give a short first-person self-report of the experience.

Do not modify production work.
